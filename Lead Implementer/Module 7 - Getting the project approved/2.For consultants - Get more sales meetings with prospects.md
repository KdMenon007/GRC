
If you are an **ISO 27001 consultant**, one of the first steps toward getting projects is increasing the number of meetings with potential clients.

The objective is to **build trust and credibility before asking prospects for a meeting**.

---

## 1. Prepare Prospects Before Contacting Them

Before directly contacting a prospect, make sure they have some understanding of:

- Who you are
    
- What services you provide
    
- Your ISO 27001 expertise
    
- The problems you can solve
    
- The benefits of working with you
    

If prospects already recognize your expertise, they are more likely to respond positively when you contact them.

---

## 2. Create an Organized Prospecting Campaign

Develop a structured approach for identifying and contacting potential customers.

### Steps:

1. Identify your target customers.
    
2. Research their business and security needs.
    
3. Identify the decision-makers.
    
4. Prepare relevant content.
    
5. Establish contact.
    
6. Build a relationship.
    
7. Request a sales/consultation meeting.
    

---

## 3. Identify Target Customers

Define the organizations that are most likely to need ISO 27001 services.

Potential prospects may include organizations that:

- Want ISO 27001 certification.
    
- Need to improve information security.
    
- Have customer requirements for ISO 27001.
    
- Need to comply with contractual requirements.
    
- Are expanding into regulated markets.
    
- Want to demonstrate security maturity.
    
- Are preparing for an upcoming certification audit.
    

---

## 4. Build Visibility

Use different channels to show prospects **who you are and what you know**.

Examples:

- Emails
    
- Newsletters
    
- Blogs
    
- Technical articles
    
- Professional networks
    
- Trade magazines
    
- Industry events
    
- Webinars
    
- Conferences
    

The goal is to establish **professional credibility** before the sales conversation.

---

## 5. Demonstrate Expertise

Speaking to potential customers through events can significantly increase your credibility.

For example:

> Speaking to 50–100 potential customers at an industry event allows them to see you as an expert in ISO 27001 and information security.

Use opportunities such as:

- Conferences
    
- Workshops
    
- Webinars
    
- Training sessions
    
- Industry events
    
- Technical presentations
    

---

## 6. Nurture Your Professional Network

Networking can generate introductions to potential customers.

You can:

- Stay connected with existing contacts.
    
- Participate in professional communities.
    
- Ask contacts for introductions.
    
- Build relationships with industry professionals.
    
- Maintain relationships even when there is no immediate sales opportunity.
    

### Example

Your existing contact knows a company preparing for ISO 27001 certification.

They can introduce you to the person responsible for the project.

This is often more effective than contacting a completely unknown prospect.

---

## 7. Share Your Knowledge

A strong way to establish credibility is to **regularly share useful information**.

You can share:

- ISO 27001 implementation experience.
    
- Lessons learned from projects.
    
- Common implementation mistakes.
    
- Risk assessment guidance.
    
- ISMS documentation tips.
    
- Audit preparation advice.
    
- Security best practices.
    
- Certification experiences.
    

---

## 8. Share Experience and Results

Don't only explain what you can do.

Show **evidence of the value you provide**.

Examples:

- Project experience
    
- Successful implementations
    
- Improvements achieved
    
- Customer outcomes
    
- Case studies
    
- Testimonials
    

### Example

Instead of simply saying:

> "We provide ISO 27001 consulting."

Show:

> "We helped an organization establish its ISMS, complete risk assessment and treatment, prepare the required documentation, and successfully prepare for certification."

---

## 9. Use Multiple Communication Channels

Useful channels include:

|Channel|Purpose|
|---|---|
|**Email**|Direct prospecting|
|**Newsletter**|Regular relationship building|
|**Blog**|Demonstrate expertise|
|**Professional networks**|Build professional visibility|
|**Trade magazines**|Reach industry-specific prospects|
|**Events**|Meet many potential customers|
|**Webinars**|Demonstrate knowledge|
|**Referrals**|Obtain warm introductions|

---

## Prospecting Flow

**Identify Target Customers**  
↓  
**Create Useful Content**  
↓  
**Build Visibility & Credibility**  
↓  
**Nurture Professional Network**  
↓  
**Get Referrals / Introductions**  
↓  
**Contact Prospects**  
↓  
**Get Sales Meetings**  
↓  
**Present ISO 27001 Services**  
↓  
**Convert Prospect into Client**

---

## Key Takeaways

- Don't immediately sell your services to completely unknown prospects.
    
- **Prepare prospects before contacting them.**
    
- Build an organized prospecting campaign.
    
- Identify the right target customers.
    
- Demonstrate your ISO 27001 expertise.
    
- Publish useful technical content.
    
- Speak at industry events.
    
- Build and nurture your professional network.
    
- Ask for introductions and referrals.
    
- Share real experience, results, and customer testimonials.
    
- Use multiple channels such as **blogs, professional networks, newsletters, trade magazines, and events**.
    

### Core Idea

> **Build credibility first → nurture relationships → get introductions → arrange sales meetings → convert prospects into clients.**
