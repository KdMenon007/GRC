
## 1. Analyze Your Present Situation

Before starting an ISO 27001 implementation project, first determine **your role and situation**.

There are three common situations:

### Situation 1 — Already Assigned to Implement ISO 27001

You work for an organization that has already:

- Assigned you responsibility for implementing the ISMS.
    
- Decided to pursue ISO 27001 certification.
    
- Given you responsibility for preparing the organization for the certification audit.
    

**Action:**  
You can skip the project-approval module and move directly to:

> **Prepare for the Implementation**

---

### Situation 2 — You Want to Initiate the Project

You already work for an organization, but management has **not yet decided** to implement and certify an ISMS.

You believe ISO 27001 implementation and certification would benefit the organization.

Your objective is to:

- Gain the interest of top management.
    
- Explain the business benefits.
    
- Obtain management approval.
    
- Initiate the ISO 27001 implementation project.
    

**Action:**  
Continue to:

> **How to Get the Interest of Top Management in the Project**

---

### Situation 3 — Working as an ISO 27001 Consultant

You work as a consultant and approach organizations that are considering:

- Implementing an ISMS.
    
- Improving their information security.
    
- Obtaining ISO 27001 certification.
    

Your role may involve:

- Understanding the organization's current situation.
    
- Explaining ISO 27001.
    
- Identifying potential benefits.
    
- Presenting the implementation approach.
    
- Helping management decide whether to start the project.
    

**Action:**  
Continue with the next units designed for consultants.

---

## Quick Decision Flow

**What is your current situation?**

➡️ **Already assigned to implement ISO 27001?**  
→ Skip project approval → **Prepare for Implementation**

➡️ **Want your organization to start ISO 27001?**  
→ Get top management interested → **Obtain project approval**

➡️ **ISO 27001 consultant?**  
→ Continue → **Work with potential client organizations**

### Key Point

Before beginning implementation, determine **whether the organization has already approved the project or whether you first need to convince top management to approve it**.
