
### 1. Review the Audit Team

The certification body will propose the **audit team**.

The organization should review:

- Auditor experience.
    
- Industry/sector experience.
    
- Auditor competence.
    
- Suitability for the organization's business.
    
- Potential conflicts of interest.
    

### Conflict of Interest

Check whether an auditor has recently:

- Worked for the organization.
    
- Provided consultancy services to the organization.
    
- Had another relationship that could affect impartiality.
    

If a potential conflict exists, inform the certification body and request a replacement if necessary.

---

## 2. Agree on Audit Dates

The certification body will propose the **audit dates**.

Audit duration is determined based on factors such as:

- Number of employees.
    
- Organization size and complexity.
    
- Number of sites.
    
- Scope of certification.
    
- Number of standards being certified.
    
- Other relevant certification requirements.
    

After agreeing on the dates, the organization can begin final audit preparations.

---

## 3. Inform the Certification Body About Special Requirements

The organization should inform the certification body about any requirements that auditors need to meet.

Examples:

- Personal protective equipment (PPE).
    
- Security clearances.
    
- Site access requirements.
    
- Confidentiality requirements.
    
- Safety procedures.
    
- Restricted areas.
    
- Special visitor requirements.
    

This should be communicated **before the audit**.

---

## 4. Provide Requested Documentation

The certification body may request documented information before the audit.

Typical information may include:

### Organization Information

- Organization chart.
    
- Site layout.
    
- Site plans.
    
- Organizational structure.
    

### Management System Information

- Management system policy.
    
- Objectives.
    
- Scope.
    
- Relevant policies and procedures.
    

### External Requirements

- Applicable standards.
    
- Relevant legislation.
    
- Regulatory requirements.
    
- Other external requirements applicable to the management system.
    

### Audit and Management Records

The certification body may also request:

- Internal audit records.
    
- Management review records.
    
- Corrective-action records.
    
- Relevant monitoring and performance records.
    

---

## 5. Review the Audit Agenda

Before the certification audit, the **Lead Auditor** normally provides an audit agenda/plan.

The agenda may specify:

- Audit dates and times.
    
- Areas to be audited.
    
- Processes to be examined.
    
- Locations to be visited.
    
- People to be interviewed.
    
- Auditors responsible for different areas.
    
- Planned audit activities.
    

---

## 6. Prepare People for the Audit

The organization should identify the employees who need to participate in the audit.

They may need to:

- Accompany auditors.
    
- Explain their processes.
    
- Provide evidence.
    
- Answer auditor questions.
    
- Demonstrate how procedures are implemented.
    

If multiple auditors are involved, the organization may need appropriate representatives to **accompany each auditor**.

---

## 7. Review and Confirm the Audit Agenda

The organization should carefully review the proposed audit agenda.

Check whether:

- All relevant areas are covered.
    
- The correct people are available.
    
- Required locations can be accessed.
    
- Timing is practical.
    
- There are any operational conflicts.
    
- Special requirements have been considered.
    

If necessary, discuss changes with the Lead Auditor.

> Auditors are generally willing to adjust the agenda when there is a legitimate operational reason.

---

## Certification Audit Preparation — Quick Checklist

- ✅ Review proposed audit team.
    
- ✅ Check auditor competence and experience.
    
- ✅ Check for conflicts of interest.
    
- ✅ Agree on audit dates.
    
- ✅ Confirm audit duration and scope.
    
- ✅ Inform auditors about special requirements.
    
- ✅ Prepare organizational charts.
    
- ✅ Prepare site layouts/plans where relevant.
    
- ✅ Prepare policies and objectives.
    
- ✅ Prepare relevant procedures.
    
- ✅ Prepare applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
    
- ✅ Prepare internal audit records.
    
- ✅ Prepare management review records.
    
- ✅ Prepare corrective-action records.
    
- ✅ Review the audit agenda.
    
- ✅ Ensure appropriate employees are available.
    
- ✅ Confirm access to required locations and information.
    

### Key Takeaway

**Certification audit preparation is not just about preparing documents.** The organization must also ensure that the **audit team is appropriate and impartial, dates and logistics are agreed, required evidence is available, relevant employees are prepared, and the audit agenda is practical and complete.**
