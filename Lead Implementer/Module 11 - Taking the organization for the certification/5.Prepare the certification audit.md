
### 1. Confirm Management System Readiness

Before the certification audit, the organization should verify that the **management system is fully prepared**.

The purpose is to ensure that:

- The ISMS has been implemented.
    
- Requirements are being followed.
    
- Controls are operating as intended.
    
- Expected results are being achieved.
    
- Necessary evidence and records are available.
    

---

## 2. Complete the Internal Audit

The organization should perform an **internal audit covering the whole management system** before the certification audit.

The internal audit helps identify:

- Nonconformities
    
- Deviations from planned arrangements
    
- Weaknesses in implementation
    
- Ineffective controls
    
- Areas requiring improvement
    

The objective is to identify problems **before the certification body does**.

---

## 3. Address Deviations

Any deviations identified during the implementation or internal audit should be reviewed and addressed.

The organization should:

- Determine what went wrong.
    
- Assess the impact.
    
- Identify the cause.
    
- Take appropriate corrective action.
    
- Verify whether the corrective action was effective.
    

Known problems should not simply be left unresolved until the certification audit.

---

## 4. Management Review Before Certification

**Top management must conduct a management review before the certification audit.**

The management review should evaluate whether the management system:

- Meets its intended requirements.
    
- Is suitable for the organization.
    
- Is adequate and effective.
    
- Has sufficient resources.
    
- Is achieving its objectives.
    
- Requires improvement.
    

Management should also make decisions about:

- Resources
    
- Improvements
    
- Objectives
    
- Corrective actions
    
- Changes required in the ISMS
    

---

## 5. Corrective Actions

The organization should not be afraid to raise corrective actions when problems are discovered.

The corrective-action process should generally involve:

**Identify problem**  
↓  
**Investigate the cause**  
↓  
**Determine root cause**  
↓  
**Define corrective action**  
↓  
**Implement corrective action**  
↓  
**Verify effectiveness**

The purpose is not simply to **fix the immediate problem**, but to address the **root cause** so that the problem does not recur.

---

## 6. Why Corrective Actions Matter

If the organization discovers a problem through its own internal audit and properly manages it through corrective action, this demonstrates that the ISMS is functioning as intended.

For example:

> Internal audit identifies that several privileged accounts do not have the required MFA.

The organization should:

1. Identify the affected accounts.
    
2. Correct the MFA issue.
    
3. Investigate why the accounts were missed.
    
4. Identify the root cause.
    
5. Improve the access-review process.
    
6. Implement corrective action.
    
7. Verify that the improvement is effective.
    

This demonstrates that the ISMS can **detect, correct, and prevent problems**.

---

## 7. Avoid Leaving Known Problems Unaddressed

A major mistake is:

> **Finding a problem internally and doing nothing about it.**

Before the certification audit, the organization should have a clear approach for every known nonconformity or significant issue.

There should be:

- Root-cause analysis.
    
- Corrective-action plan.
    
- Assigned responsibility.
    
- Target completion date.
    
- Evidence of implementation.
    
- Effectiveness verification.
    

---

## 8. Certification Readiness Checklist

Before the certification audit, verify:

- ✅ Full ISMS internal audit completed.
    
- ✅ Internal audit findings documented.
    
- ✅ Deviations identified and addressed.
    
- ✅ Corrective actions initiated where necessary.
    
- ✅ Root causes investigated.
    
- ✅ Effectiveness of corrective actions verified where applicable.
    
- ✅ Management review completed.
    
- ✅ Required resources provided.
    
- ✅ ISMS objectives reviewed.
    
- ✅ Required evidence and records available.
    
- ✅ Known problems are not being ignored.
    
- ✅ Management system is operating in practice.
    

### Key Takeaway

**Do not wait for the certification auditor to discover problems that your organization already knows about.**

The purpose of the internal audit and management review is to **find problems early, correct their root causes, verify effectiveness, and demonstrate that the ISMS is capable of continual improvement before the certification audit begins.**
