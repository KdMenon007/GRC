
At the beginning of an ISO 27001 implementation project, team members need to understand the **standard, project objectives, and their responsibilities**. Training and documentation should then continue throughout the project in a practical and organization-specific way.

### 1. Initial Training

At the start of the project, provide team members with a **general overview of the applicable management system standard**.

Training should help them understand:

- Purpose of the standard
    
- Main requirements
    
- Project objectives
    
- Their roles and responsibilities
    
- How they will contribute to implementation
    

---

### 2. Specific and Tailored Training

During implementation, provide **short, topic-specific training sessions** before starting work on a particular area.

For example:

- Before developing procurement procedures → explain relevant procurement requirements.
    
- Before implementing access control → explain access control requirements.
    
- Before developing incident management → explain incident management requirements.
    

This makes training directly relevant to the work being performed.

---

### 3. Project Manager's Role in Documentation

The project manager helps team members develop:

- Policies
    
- Procedures
    
- Forms
    
- Templates
    
- Work instructions
    
- Other required documented information
    

Templates can be provided to make documentation easier and faster.

---

### 4. Do Not Copy Documents from Other Organizations

A major principle is:

> **Do not simply copy another organization's policies and procedures.**

Documents should be **tailored to the organization's actual situation**.

Consider:

- Organization size
    
- Business processes
    
- Risks
    
- Products and services
    
- Organizational structure
    
- Legal requirements
    
- Existing practices
    
- Available resources
    

A document that works for a large organization may be unnecessary or impractical for a small organization.

---

### 5. Co-Creation of Documents

Documents should be developed together with the **people who will actually use them**.

Where possible, involve:

- Process owners
    
- Employees
    
- Managers
    
- Subject-matter experts
    
- Project team members
    

#### Benefits

Employee participation helps to:

- Reduce resistance to change
    
- Increase commitment
    
- Improve understanding
    
- Make procedures more practical
    
- Increase acceptance of new processes
    
- Create a sense of ownership
    

Employees are more likely to follow a procedure when they understand **why it exists and participated in creating it**.

---

### 6. Appropriate Amount of Documentation

ISO 27001 does **not** require every organization to have the same number or type of documents.

The organization should create the **appropriate level of documentation** needed to operate and control the ISMS effectively.

Avoid:

- Unnecessary documents
    
- Excessively long procedures
    
- Documents created only for the audit
    
- Complicated processes that employees will not follow
    

---

### 7. Mandatory vs. Non-Mandatory Documentation

#### Mandatory Documentation

Where ISO 27001 requires documented information, the organization must establish and maintain it as applicable.

#### Non-Mandatory Documentation

Additional documents are optional and should be created when they provide value.

Examples:

- Detailed procedures
    
- Work instructions
    
- Checklists
    
- Additional forms
    
- Guidance documents
    

The organization should decide whether these documents are actually useful.

---

### 8. Documentation Depends on Organization Size

**Larger organizations** generally require more documentation because they may have:

- More employees
    
- Higher employee turnover
    
- More complex processes
    
- Multiple locations
    
- More products and services
    
- More detailed operational requirements
    

**Smaller organizations** generally have:

- Fewer documents
    
- Simpler procedures
    
- Shorter documents
    
- Less complex processes
    

### Key Principle

> **The amount of documentation should be appropriate to the organization's size, complexity, risks, and operational needs.**

---

## ISO 27001 Implementation Approach

A practical approach is:

**Train → Understand → Co-create → Document → Implement → Review → Improve**

The goal is **not to create as many documents as possible**. The goal is to create documentation that is **useful, understood by employees, implemented in practice, and appropriate for the organization's ISMS**.
