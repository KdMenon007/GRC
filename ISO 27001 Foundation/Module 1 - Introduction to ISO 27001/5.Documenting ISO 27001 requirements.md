
## Overview

Documentation is a critical component of an **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS)**.

ISO 27001 requires organizations to maintain **documented information** that demonstrates:

- How the ISMS is established
    
- How security risks are managed
    
- How controls are implemented
    
- How security performance is monitored
    
- How continual improvement is achieved
    

Documentation provides evidence that the organization follows a **structured and risk-based approach** to information security.

---

# Purpose of ISO 27001 Documentation

Proper documentation helps organizations:

- Establish clear security processes
    
- Define responsibilities
    
- Demonstrate compliance
    
- Provide audit evidence
    
- Maintain consistency
    
- Support continual improvement
    

Documentation should be:

- Controlled
    
- Updated regularly
    
- Accessible to authorized personnel
    
- Protected from unauthorized changes
    

---

# Key ISO 27001 Documents

---

# 1. Information Security Management System (ISMS) Policy

## Purpose

The **ISMS Policy** defines the organization's commitment and direction toward information security.

It is a high-level document approved by top management.

---

## Contents

The policy includes:

- Organization's security commitment
    
- Information security objectives
    
- Security principles
    
- Compliance requirements
    
- Management responsibilities
    
- Commitment to continual improvement
    

---

## Example

> "The organization is committed to protecting information assets by maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability through effective security controls and risk management."

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 5.2 – Information Security Policy**

---

# 2. Scope of ISMS

## Purpose

Defines the boundaries and applicability of the Information Security Management System.

It explains:

- What is included
    
- What is excluded
    
- Which assets are protected
    
- Which processes are covered
    

---

## Scope Includes

- Business units
    
- Locations
    
- Applications
    
- Infrastructure
    
- Information assets
    
- Supporting processes
    

---

## Example

**ISMS Scope:**

"The ISMS covers customer applications, cloud infrastructure, databases, and supporting IT operations."

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 4.3 – Determining the Scope of the ISMS**

---

# 3. Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment Documentation

## Purpose

Documents how the organization identifies, analyzes, and manages information security risks.

Risk management is the foundation of ISO 27001.

---

# Risk Assessment Documentation Includes:

## 1. Risk Identification

Identifies:

- Assets
    
- Threats
    
- Vulnerabilities
    
- Existing controls
    

Example:

|Asset|Threat|Vulnerability|
|---|---|---|
|Customer Database|Data breach|Weak access control|

---

## 2. Risk Analysis

Evaluates:

- Likelihood
    
- Impact
    
- Risk level
    

Example:

```
Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact
```

---

## 3. Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)

Defines:

- Treatment approach
    
- Security controls
    
- Responsible owner
    
- Implementation timeline
    

---

## Risk Treatment Options

|Option|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Avoid|Remove the risky activity|
|Reduce|Implement controls|
|Transfer|Share risk with another party|
|Accept|Accept remaining risk|

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 6.1 – Actions to Address Risks and Opportunities**

---

# 4. Statement of Applicability (SoA)

## Purpose

The Statement of Applicability is a key ISO 27001 document that explains which Annex A controls are applicable and why.

---

## SoA Contains:

- Applicable controls
    
- Excluded controls
    
- Justification for exclusions
    
- Implementation status
    

---

## Example

|Annex A Control|Applicable|Reason|
|---|---|---|
|Access Control|Yes|Required to protect user accounts|
|Physical Security|Yes|Protect data center|
|Supplier Security|No|No external suppliers|

---

## Importance

The SoA connects:

```
Risk Assessment
       ↓
Risk Treatment
       ↓
Selected Controls
       ↓
Statement of Applicability
```

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 6.1.3 – Information Security Risk Treatment**

---

# 5. Information Security Controls Documentation

## Purpose

Documents security measures implemented to reduce identified risks.

Controls can be:

---

# Administrative Controls

Examples:

- Security policies
    
- Procedures
    
- Guidelines
    
- Employee training
    

---

# Technical Controls

Examples:

- Firewall rules
    
- SIEM monitoring
    
- Encryption
    
- Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)
    
- Multi-factor authentication
    

---

# Physical Controls

Examples:

- CCTV
    
- Access cards
    
- Data center security
    

---

## Documentation Examples

- Access Control Policy
    
- Incident Response Procedure
    
- Backup Procedure
    
- Vulnerability Management Procedure
    
- Change Management Procedure
    

---

# 6. Roles and Responsibilities Documentation

## Purpose

Defines who is responsible for managing and performing ISMS activities.

Clear ownership prevents confusion and improves accountability.

---

## Example Roles

|Role|Responsibility|
|---|---|
|Top Management|Strategic direction and resources|
|CISO|Security governance|
|ISMS Manager|ISMS maintenance|
|Risk Owner|Risk decisions|
|Asset Owner|Asset protection|
|SOC Analyst|Monitoring and incident response|

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 5.3 – Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities**

---

# 7. Training and Awareness Documentation

## Purpose

Ensures employees understand their security responsibilities.

Security awareness reduces human-related risks.

---

## Documentation Includes:

- Training plans
    
- Attendance records
    
- Awareness materials
    
- Assessment results
    

---

## Training Examples:

- Phishing awareness
    
- Password security
    
- Data protection
    
- Incident reporting
    
- Acceptable use policy
    

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 7.2 – Competence**  
**Clause 7.3 – Awareness**

---

# 8. Monitoring and Measurement Documentation

## Purpose

Measures whether the ISMS and security controls are effective.

---

## Includes:

### Security Metrics

Examples:

- Number of security incidents
    
- Vulnerability closure rate
    
- Patch compliance
    
- Incident response time
    

---

### Internal Audit Records

Includes:

- Audit plans
    
- Audit reports
    
- Findings
    
- Corrective actions
    

---

### Performance Tracking

Measures:

- Security objectives
    
- Control effectiveness
    
- Risk status
    

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 9.1 – Monitoring, Measurement, Analysis and Evaluation**

---

# 9. Management Review Documentation

## Purpose

Demonstrates that top management regularly evaluates ISMS effectiveness.

---

## Management Reviews Include:

- Audit results
    
- Security incidents
    
- Risk status
    
- Security objectives
    
- Improvement opportunities
    
- Resource requirements
    

---

## Output of Management Review

Management may decide:

- Update policies
    
- Improve controls
    
- Allocate additional resources
    
- Modify objectives
    

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 9.3 – Management Review**

---

# 10. Records Management Documentation

## Purpose

Defines how ISMS records are created, stored, protected, and retained.

Records provide evidence that security processes are being followed.

---

## Records Examples:

- Risk assessment reports
    
- Audit reports
    
- Training records
    
- Incident reports
    
- Access review records
    
- Change records
    
- Management review minutes
    

---

## Record Management Requirements

Organizations must define:

- Storage location
    
- Retention period
    
- Access permissions
    
- Protection methods
    
- Disposal process
    

---

## ISO 27001 Reference

**Clause 7.5 – Documented Information**
