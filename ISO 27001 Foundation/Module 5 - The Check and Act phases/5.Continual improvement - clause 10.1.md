
**Purpose:**  
ISO 27001 requires the organization to **continually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the ISMS**.

### Why continual improvement is required

The ISMS cannot remain static because:

- Business processes change.
    
- Information security risks change.
    
- Threats and vulnerabilities change.
    
- Security controls may become less effective.
    
- Technology changes.
    
- Legal and regulatory requirements change.
    
- ISO 27001 itself can be updated.
    

Therefore, the ISMS must continuously adapt to the changing environment.

### What should be improved?

Examples include:

- Implementing security tools to automate activities.
    
- Improving incident management processes.
    
- Creating or updating security procedures.
    
- Introducing better security awareness programs.
    
- Improving risk assessment methods.
    
- Strengthening security controls.
    
- Improving monitoring and measurement.
    
- Automating compliance or reporting activities.
    

### How does continual improvement work?

A practical improvement process can be:

**Identify improvement opportunity → Plan improvement → Implement change → Measure results → Review effectiveness → Further improve**

For example:

> The organization notices that employees frequently fail phishing simulations → improve security awareness training → introduce monthly phishing simulations → measure employee performance → review results → improve the training program.

### Responsibilities

The organization should establish who is responsible for:

- Planning improvement activities.
    
- Managing and coordinating improvements.
    
- Implementing improvements.
    
- Recording improvement activities.
    
- Reviewing whether improvements were effective.
    

The **CISO/ISMS Manager** commonly coordinates these activities, while management provides direction, resources, and approval where necessary.

### Employee involvement

Continual improvement should not be limited to the CISO or security team. Employees should be encouraged to identify and suggest improvements because they work with the organization's processes and controls every day.

### Important point

**Not every change is an improvement.**

A change may be implemented but fail to produce the expected security benefit. Therefore, the organization should **measure and review the effectiveness of improvements**.

### Documentation

ISO 27001 does **not specifically require a separate continual improvement procedure**. However, organizations may maintain:

- Improvement register
    
- Improvement action plan
    
- Meeting minutes
    
- Corrective action records
    
- Risk assessment updates
    
- ISMS change records
    
- Management review records
