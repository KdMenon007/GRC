
Clause **9.2** is part of the **Check phase of the PDCA cycle**. Its purpose is to provide an **independent and objective assessment** of whether the ISMS is working as intended and meeting ISO 27001 requirements.

### 1. Purpose of Internal Audit

The internal audit determines whether:

- The ISMS meets the **organization's own requirements**.
    
- The ISMS meets the requirements of **ISO/IEC 27001**.
    
- The ISMS is **effectively implemented and maintained**.
    
- The ISMS can be **improved**.
    

The audit provides management with an objective view of what is actually happening within the ISMS.

---

## 2. Internal Audit Program

The organization must conduct internal audits at **planned intervals**.

An **internal audit program** defines the schedule for audits.

It should consider:

- Frequency of audits
    
- Scope of audits
    
- Processes/departments to be audited
    
- Previous audit results
    
- Importance of processes
    
- Significant organizational changes
    
- Risks associated with the processes
    

### Example

A large organization might plan:

- **June:** IT operations and access management
    
- **September:** HR security and supplier management
    
- **December:** Incident management and business continuity
    

A small organization might audit the entire ISMS in one audit.

---

## 3. Audit Scope

The **audit scope** defines what will be examined.

It can include:

- Business processes
    
- Departments
    
- Locations
    
- Information systems
    
- Security controls
    
- ISMS processes
    

The scope should be defined in the audit program/planning documentation.

---

## 4. Audit Criteria

The auditor needs clear criteria against which the organization will be assessed.

Examples:

- **ISO/IEC 27001 requirements**
    
- Organization's ISMS policies and procedures
    
- Legal and regulatory requirements
    
- Contractual requirements
    
- Requirements from interested parties
    

### Example

If auditing access control, the auditor might compare actual practices against:

> ISO 27001 requirements + Access Control Policy + applicable legal/contractual requirements.

---

## 5. Auditor Independence

One of the most important requirements is **objectivity and impartiality**.

> **An auditor should not audit their own work.**

For example:

❌ The IT administrator who implemented access controls audits those same controls.

✅ Another qualified employee or an external auditor performs the audit.

This prevents **conflict of interest** and improves the reliability of audit results.

---

## 6. Auditor Competence

Internal auditors should have appropriate knowledge and skills, including:

- ISO 27001 knowledge
    
- Information security knowledge
    
- Auditing techniques
    
- Interviewing skills
    
- Evidence collection
    
- Reporting and documentation
    

Organizations may provide **internal auditor training** to employees.

---

## 7. Internal Audit Plan

An individual audit plan is **good practice**, even though ISO 27001 does not necessarily require a specific document called an "audit plan."

It can define:

- Audit scope
    
- Audit criteria
    
- Audit objectives
    
- Audit date and duration
    
- Auditors
    
- Processes/departments being audited
    
- People to interview
    
- Evidence to examine
    

This helps ensure that important areas are not missed.

---

## 8. Conducting the Audit

During the audit, the auditor gathers **objective evidence** through activities such as:

- Interviews
    
- Document reviews
    
- Observation
    
- Sampling
    
- Reviewing system records
    
- Checking logs
    
- Testing controls
    

### Example

The policy says:

> All privileged accounts must use MFA.

The auditor may:

1. Review the policy.
    
2. Obtain a list of privileged accounts.
    
3. Check whether MFA is enabled.
    
4. Review relevant system configuration.
    
5. Record the evidence.
    
6. Determine whether the requirement is being followed.
    

---

## 9. Audit Findings

The audit may identify:

### Conformities

The organization is meeting the requirement.

### Nonconformities

A requirement is not being fulfilled.

### Good practices

An effective practice that is worth highlighting.

### Opportunities for improvement

Areas where the ISMS could potentially become more effective or efficient.

---

## 10. Internal Audit Report

The audit results must be **reported to relevant management**.

A typical internal audit report may contain:

- Audit objective
    
- Audit scope
    
- Audit criteria
    
- Audit date
    
- Auditors
    
- Processes audited
    
- Evidence reviewed
    
- Findings
    
- Nonconformities
    
- Good practices
    
- Opportunities for improvement
    
- Recommendations
    
- Required follow-up actions
    

The results become an important input for **management review (Clause 9.3)** and continual improvement.

---

## CISO's Role in Internal Audit

The **CISO may coordinate or oversee the internal audit program**, but the CISO should not automatically audit his/her own work.

The CISO may:

- Ensure an internal audit program is established.
    
- Help identify high-risk areas requiring audit.
    
- Ensure appropriate audit scope and criteria.
    
- Ensure competent and independent auditors are selected.
    
- Review audit findings.
    
- Coordinate corrective actions.
    
- Track remediation of nonconformities.
    
- Report significant audit results to top management.
    
- Use audit results to improve the ISMS.
    

### Important

If the CISO is responsible for implementing a particular security process, **the CISO should not audit that same work** because of the independence/objectivity requirement. Another competent internal auditor or an external auditor should perform it.

---

## Documentation

ISO 27001 requires documented information as evidence of the **implementation of the audit program and the audit results**.

Typical records include:

- Internal audit program
    
- Audit plans
    
- Audit reports
    
- Audit findings
    
- Evidence/evaluation records
    
- Corrective-action follow-up
