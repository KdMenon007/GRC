### Purpose

The organization must determine whether its **information security controls and ISMS are performing effectively**.

### Four key activities

1. **Monitoring** – Continuously observe or review security activities.
    
    - Example: Monitor security incidents.
        
2. **Measurement** – Determine results using measurable values.
    
    - Example: Number of security incidents per week.
        
    - Example: Average time to resolve an incident.
        
3. **Analysis** – Examine and compare the measurement results.
    
    - Example: Compare the number of incidents across different weeks.
        
4. **Evaluation** – Draw conclusions and decide whether action is needed.
    
    - Example: If incidents are consistently increasing, investigate the reason and take corrective action.
        

### What must be defined?

The organization should determine:

|Requirement|Example|CISO's role|
|---|---|---|
|**What to monitor**|Incidents, vulnerabilities, controls|Defines/oversees relevant security metrics|
|**What to measure**|Incident count, resolution time|Establishes KPIs/KRIs|
|**Method**|SIEM reports, vulnerability reports|Ensures appropriate measurement methods|
|**Frequency**|Real-time, weekly, monthly|Defines/approves reporting frequency|
|**Responsible person**|SOC Analyst, IT Admin|Assigns/coordinates responsibilities|
|**Analysis**|Identify trends and deviations|Reviews and analyzes security results|
|**Evaluation**|Determine effectiveness|Evaluates ISMS/control effectiveness|
|**Action**|Improve controls|Recommends corrective/improvement actions|
### Example — Incident Management

|Activity|Example|
|---|---|
|What|Incident management process|
|Monitoring|Review incidents in the incident-management system|
|Measurement|Average incident-resolution time|
|Frequency|Real-time monitoring, weekly measurement|
|Analysis|CISO analyzes weekly results|
|Evaluation|Management reviews trends monthly|
|Evidence|Incident reports and measurement results|

### CISO's overall responsibility

The **CISO typically coordinates and oversees Clause 9.1 activities**, although the standard does **not require the CISO specifically** to perform every activity.

The CISO may:

1. Define relevant **security KPIs/KRIs**.
2. Establish monitoring and measurement requirements.
3. Coordinate data collection from SOC, IT, HR, risk management, etc.
4. Review security metrics and trends.
5. Evaluate whether controls are effective.
6. Report significant results to **top management**.
7. Recommend corrective actions and improvements.
8. Use the results as input for the **Management Review (Clause 9.3)** and **continual improvement (Clause 10)**.

### Documentation

The organization must retain **documented information as evidence of the monitoring and measurement results**.

Examples:

- Security KPI reports
- Incident reports
- Vulnerability reports
- Audit results
- Security dashboards
- Risk-monitoring reports
- Control-performance reports

A separate **Monitoring and Measurement Procedure** is not necessarily mandatory, but it is good practice, particularly for larger organizations.

### Why is Clause 9.1 important?

The results provide evidence of whether the ISMS is **effective** and become an important input for **management review and continual improvement**.
