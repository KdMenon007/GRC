
The **Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)** is the practical implementation plan for the controls selected through the **risk treatment process and documented in the Statement of Applicability (SoA)**.

Its purpose is to define **exactly how the selected controls will be implemented**, including responsibility, deadlines, resources, and follow-up.

---

## 1. What should the RTP contain?

The RTP should primarily list the **controls from the SoA that have not yet been implemented**, together with the details needed to implement them.

A typical RTP can contain:

|RTP Element|What it means|
|---|---|
|**Control / Action**|What security control needs to be implemented|
|**Related Risk**|The risk that led to selecting the control|
|**Responsible Person**|Person accountable for implementation|
|**Deadline**|Target date for implementation|
|**Resources**|Budget, people, technology, external expertise, etc.|
|**Status**|Planned, In Progress, Completed, etc.|
|**Follow-up / Result**|Outcome of implementation and verification|

---

# 2. Example Risk Treatment Plan

Suppose the risk assessment identified:

> **Risk:** Disclosure of confidential information through insecure email communication.

The organization decides to implement appropriate cryptographic controls.

The RTP could look like this:

|Control / Action|Related Risk|Responsible Person|Deadline|Resources|Follow-up|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Develop Cryptographic Policy|Confidential information disclosure|CISO|30 Sep 2026|Security team|Policy approved|
|Implement email encryption|Email eavesdropping|IT Manager|31 Oct 2026|Encryption solution + IT resources|Encryption tested|
|Configure secure key management|Unauthorized access to encrypted data|Security Architect|30 Nov 2026|Technical expertise|Configuration verified|

# 3. Relationship Between Risk, SoA and RTP

These three documents/processes are closely connected.

```text
Risk Assessment
       ↓
Identify unacceptable risk
       ↓
Risk Treatment Decision
       ↓
Select Security Controls
       ↓
Statement of Applicability (SoA)
       ↓
Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)
       ↓
Implement Controls
       ↓
Verify Effectiveness
```

### Easy way to remember:

**Risk Assessment → What is wrong?**

**Risk Treatment → What should we do?**

**SoA → Which controls are applicable?**

**RTP → How, who, when, and with what resources?**

**Implementation → Actually put the controls into operation.**

---

# 4. Why are Risk Owners Important?

After preparing the RTP, it is important to obtain **approval from the relevant risk owners**.

The **risk owner** is the person responsible for managing a particular risk and ensuring that the chosen treatment is appropriate.

For example:

> Risk: Customer database compromise  
> Risk Owner: Head of IT

The Head of IT should agree with the proposed treatment because implementation may require:

- Budget
    
- Staff time
    
- Infrastructure changes
    
- New software
    
- Changes to business processes
    
- Coordination with other departments
    

If the risk owner does not agree, implementation can face problems.

### Possible problems

- Implementation delays
    
- Insufficient budget
    
- Conflicts with other projects
    
- Lack of resources
    
- Controls that do not fit business requirements
    
- Unclear responsibilities
    

Therefore:

> **Risk owner approval helps ensure that the proposed risk treatment is realistic, supported, and implementable.**

---

# 5. RTP vs SoA

This is an important distinction for ISO 27001 exams and interviews.

|Statement of Applicability|Risk Treatment Plan|
|---|---|
|Defines applicable controls|Defines implementation activities|
|Explains why controls are applicable|Explains how controls will be implemented|
|Justifies exclusions|Assigns responsibility|
|Shows control implementation status|Defines deadlines and resources|
|Control-oriented|Action-oriented|
|**Selection**|**Execution**|

### Example

**SoA:**

> Cryptographic controls are applicable because the organization processes confidential information and must protect data during transmission.

**RTP:**

> IT Security Manager will implement email encryption by 31 October 2026 using the approved encryption solution, with a budget of ₹X and verification through security testing.

---

# 6. RTP and the PDCA Cycle

The RTP is particularly important because it connects the **Plan** phase to the **Do** phase.

```text
PLAN
 ├── Risk Assessment
 ├── Risk Treatment
 ├── Control Selection
 ├── SoA
 └── Risk Treatment Plan
             ↓
DO
 └── Implement Selected Controls
             ↓
CHECK
 └── Monitor / Audit / Measure
             ↓
ACT
 └── Corrective Action / Improvement
```

So the RTP is essentially the **implementation roadmap for risk treatment**.
