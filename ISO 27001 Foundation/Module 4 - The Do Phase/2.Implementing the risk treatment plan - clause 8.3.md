
After the **Risk Assessment → Risk Treatment → SoA → Risk Treatment Plan**, the next step is to actually **implement the selected controls**.

This is part of the **DO phase of the PDCA cycle** and corresponds primarily to **ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 8 – Operation**.

---

## 1. What does implementation mean?

Implementation means putting the controls identified in the Risk Treatment Plan into actual operation.

The organization must implement the controls while considering:

- Available resources
    
- Assigned responsibilities
    
- Implementation deadlines
    
- Approved budget
    
- Technical requirements
    
- Business requirements
    
- Dependencies on other projects
    

Simply selecting a control is not enough.

> **A control only provides security value when it is actually implemented and operating effectively.**

---

## 2. Examples of Control Implementation

Implementation can take different forms.

### Policies and procedures

For example:

- Information Security Policy
    
- Password Policy
    
- Cryptographic Policy
    
- Access Control Procedure
    
- Incident Response Procedure
    
- Backup Procedure
    

### Technical controls

For example:

- Implementing MFA
    
- Deploying antivirus/EDR
    
- Configuring firewalls
    
- Encrypting sensitive information
    
- Implementing backups
    
- Configuring SIEM monitoring
    

### Organizational processes

For example:

- Establishing an access-review process
    
- Creating an incident management process
    
- Conducting security awareness training
    
- Performing supplier security assessments
    
- Establishing vulnerability management

# 3. Example

Suppose the Risk Treatment Plan contains:

|Risk|Control|Responsible|Deadline|
|---|---|---|---|
|Malware infection on servers|Implement endpoint protection|IT Administrator|30 Sep 2026|

The IT Administrator must actually:

1. Select/obtain the approved endpoint protection solution.
    
2. Install it on the relevant servers.
    
3. Configure the security policies.
    
4. Verify that protection is functioning.
    
5. Document the implementation.
    
6. Report completion to the responsible person.
    

The control is now **implemented**, rather than simply being listed in the RTP.

---

# 4. Documenting the Results

ISO 27001 requires the organization to retain **documented information as evidence of the results of risk treatment**.

For example:

```text
Risk Treatment Plan
        ↓
Control Implementation
        ↓
Implementation Evidence
        ↓
Update RTP / Risk Records
        ↓
Management Review
```

Evidence could include:

- Completed implementation records
    
- Configuration records
    
- Approval records
    
- Test results
    
- Screenshots
    
- Training records
    
- Audit evidence
    
- Implementation reports
    
- Updated policies/procedures
    

The exact form of evidence depends on the control.

---

# 5. Example – Antivirus Control

Suppose the RTP says:

> **Implement antivirus protection on production servers.**

### Before implementation

**Status:** Planned

### During implementation

The IT administrator:

- Installs the antivirus/EDR solution.
    
- Configures policies.
    
- Enables automatic updates.
    
- Verifies detection capability.
    
- Performs testing.
    

### After implementation

The administrator reports:

> "Endpoint protection has been successfully installed and configured on all identified production servers."

The organization records the result.

**Status:** Completed

**Evidence:** Deployment report/configuration record/test results.

# 6. Management Review

The **status and results of risk treatment** should be considered during the **Management Review**.

Management can review:

- Which controls have been implemented
    
- Which controls are delayed
    
- Which activities are still pending
    
- Whether the treatment achieved its intended result
    
- Whether additional resources are required
    
- Whether residual risks remain
    
- Whether further actions are necessary
    

For example:

|Control|Planned Date|Status|Result|
|---|---|---|---|
|MFA implementation|30 Sep|Completed|MFA enabled|
|EDR deployment|15 Oct|In Progress|80% deployed|
|Backup improvement|30 Oct|Delayed|Vendor dependency|

Management can then decide whether additional resources or corrective actions are required.

---

# 7. Important Distinction

Don't confuse **implementation** with **effectiveness**.

### Implementation

> **Did we put the control into operation?**

Example:

> EDR was installed on all servers.

### Effectiveness

> **Is the control actually achieving its intended security objective?**

Example:

> EDR successfully detects and blocks relevant malware and alerts the SOC.

The effectiveness of controls becomes particularly important during **monitoring, measurement, internal audit, and management review**.

# 8. Position in the ISO 27001 Lifecycle

```text
PLAN
│
├── Understand Context
├── Identify Risks
├── Analyze & Evaluate Risks
├── Select Risk Treatment
├── Prepare SoA
└── Prepare Risk Treatment Plan
            ↓
        DO / OPERATION
            ↓
    Implement Controls
            ↓
    Record Results/Evidence
            ↓
        CHECK
            ↓
    Monitor & Measure
    Internal Audit
    Management Review
            ↓
        ACT
            ↓
    Corrective Action
    Continual Improvement
```

### Easy memory trick

**PLAN = Decide**

> What risks exist? What controls do we need? Who will implement them?

**DO = Implement**

> Put the controls into practice.

**CHECK = Verify**

> Are the controls working and is the ISMS effective?

**ACT = Improve**

> Fix problems and make improvements.
