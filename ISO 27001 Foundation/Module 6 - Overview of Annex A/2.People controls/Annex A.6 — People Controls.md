## Annex A.6 — People Controls

**A.6 People Controls** is the shortest Annex A section, containing **8 controls**. It focuses on managing information security risks related to people throughout the **entire employment lifecycle**.

### Main areas

|Stage|Focus|
|---|---|
|**Before employment**|Screening and employment terms|
|**During employment**|Awareness, training, responsibilities, disciplinary process|
|**Change/termination**|Responsibilities after role changes or leaving the organization|

### Why A.6 is important

People can be a major source of information security risk. Security incidents may result from:

- Human error
    
- Lack of security awareness
    
- Weak password practices
    
- Phishing
    
- Accidental data disclosure
    
- Improper handling of information
    
- Intentional malicious activity
    

Therefore, A.6 ensures that people understand their **security responsibilities** and follow the organization's security requirements.

### Practical implementation

Organizations can implement A.6 controls through:

- **Employment agreements** containing information security responsibilities.
    
- **Confidentiality/NDA agreements** for employees and relevant external parties.
    
- **Background screening** where appropriate and legally permitted.
    
- **Security awareness training**.
    
- **Role-specific security training**.
    
- **Secure remote-working requirements**.
    
- **Disciplinary procedures** for security violations.
    
- Removing or changing access and responsibilities when an employee **changes role or leaves**.
    

### Simple lifecycle

**Screen → Hire → Define responsibilities → Train & create awareness → Monitor/discipline → Role change/termination → Remove or modify access**
