
### A.5.9 Inventory of Information and Other Associated Assets

**Purpose:**  
To establish rules for **identifying and maintaining an inventory of organizational assets**, including hardware, software, documents, and other information.

**Key points:**

- The organization should identify its assets.
    
- Assets include:
    
    - Hardware
        
    - Software
        
    - Documents
        
    - Other information
        
- An **asset inventory** can be created to maintain a list of assets.
    
- An asset-based risk assessment can make this easier because the organization already has a list of assets from the risk assessment.
    
- Each asset should have an **owner**.
    
- The asset owner is responsible for the **handling and maintenance** of the asset.
    

**Implementation example:**

Create an asset inventory containing:

|Asset|Owner|
|---|---|
|Server|IT Manager|
|Laptop|Assigned Employee|
|Database|Database Administrator|
|Customer Documents|Business/Information Owner|

**Key takeaway:**

> The organization must know **what assets it has and who owns them**.

### A.5.10 Acceptable Use of Information and Other Associated Assets

**Purpose:**  
To define rules for the **secure and acceptable use of organizational assets**.

**Key points:**

- The organization should define how assets must be used.
    
- Rules should consider:
    
    - Security risks
        
    - Asset classification
        
- The rules should be documented in a **policy**.
    
- Acceptable-use rules can cover both:
    
    - **Technical requirements**
        
    - **Organizational requirements**
        

**Examples:**

- How to perform a backup.
    
- How to request permission to use an asset outside company premises.
    
- How assets should be handled according to their classification.
    

**Key takeaway:**

> Acceptable Use defines **how employees are allowed to use organizational assets securely**.

---

### A.5.11 Return of Assets

**Purpose:**  
To ensure that organizational assets are **returned when they are no longer required**, particularly when an employee leaves the organization.

**Key requirement:**

- Assets owned by the organization must be returned when the **employment contract is terminated**.
    

**Examples of assets that may need to be returned:**

- Company laptops
    
- Hardware
    
- Documents
    
- Other organizational information/assets
    

**Key takeaway:**

> When employment ends, **company-owned assets must be returned**.
