
The **Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)** is the document that turns the decisions made during **risk assessment and risk treatment** into concrete implementation activities.

It answers the practical question:

> **“What are we going to do about the identified risks, who will do it, when, and with what resources?”**

### 1. Where does the RTP fit?

```text
Risk Assessment
      ↓
Identify Risks
      ↓
Analyze & Evaluate Risks
      ↓
Select Risk Treatment Options
      ↓
Select Controls
      ↓
Statement of Applicability (SoA)
      ↓
Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)
      ↓
Implement Controls
```

The **SoA** tells us **which controls have been selected and why**.

The **RTP** tells us **how those controls will actually be implemented**.

---

## 2. Purpose of the Risk Treatment Plan

The RTP provides a structured implementation plan for treating unacceptable risks.

It normally identifies:

- What control/action needs to be implemented
    
- Which risk it addresses
    
- Who is responsible
    
- What activities need to be performed
    
- Implementation deadline
    
- Required resources
    
- Required budget
    
- Implementation priority
    
- Current status
    
- Expected completion date
    

---

## 3. Example

Suppose the risk assessment identifies:

**Risk:** Server becomes unavailable because of power failure.

**Risk treatment:** Reduce the risk.

**Selected control:** Implement backup power protection.

The RTP could look like:

|Item|Details|
|---|---|
|Risk|Server unavailability due to power failure|
|Treatment|Reduce|
|Control|Backup power / UPS|
|Action|Purchase and configure UPS|
|Responsible Person|IT Infrastructure Manager|
|Budget|₹2,00,000|
|Deadline|30 September 2026|
|Priority|High|
|Status|In Progress|
|Verification|Test server operation during simulated power failure|

---

## 4. RTP and PDCA

The Risk Treatment Plan is particularly important because it connects **Plan** with **Do**.

```text
PLAN
Risk Assessment
     ↓
Risk Treatment
     ↓
SoA
     ↓
Risk Treatment Plan
     ↓
-------------------------
DO
     ↓
Implement Controls
```

So:

> **SoA = What controls are applicable and why?**

> **RTP = How will those controls be implemented?**

---

## 5. Difference Between SoA and RTP

|SoA|Risk Treatment Plan|
|---|---|
|Identifies applicable controls|Defines implementation activities|
|Explains why controls are applicable|Defines who will implement them|
|Justifies exclusions|Defines deadlines|
|Shows implementation status|Defines resources and budget|
|Control-focused|Action/implementation-focused|
|Supports audit and control selection|Supports execution of risk treatment|

### Simple Memory Trick

**SoA = Selection**

**RTP = Execution Plan**
