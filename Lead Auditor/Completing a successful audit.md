
## Audit Findings

### 1. Objective Assessment

- As a lead auditor, focus on providing an honest and thorough assessment, not on the number of non-conformities found.

### 2. Major vs. Minor Non-Conformities

#### a. Major Non-Conformity:

- Occurs when a process fails to achieve its intended outcomes.
- **Example:** An organization has a procedure but does not conduct management reviews (ISO 9001 clause 9.3).

#### b. Minor Non-Conformity:

- Occurs when a requirement is not met, but the intended outcomes are still achievable.
- **Example:** Some employees haven’t completed mandatory training but are still competent (ISO 9001 clause 7.2).

### 3. Multiple Minor Non-Conformities

- If multiple minor non-conformities are related to the same requirement (e.g., training issues), they may be considered as one major non-conformity.

## How to Reach Audit Conclusions

### 1. Audit Conclusions vs. Audit Results

- **Audit results** list non-conformities.
- **Audit conclusions** assess how well the management system meets the standard’s requirements.
- Focus on long-term systemic improvements.

### 2. Key Elements of Audit Conclusion

- Whether the management system is effective, maintained, and improving.
- The level of conformance with audit criteria.
- How well the system achieves its stated objectives.

### 3. Handling Major Non-Conformities

- Certification **cannot** be granted until corrections and corrective actions are reviewed, accepted, and verified by the certification body.
- A special audit may be required.

### 4. Handling Minor Non-Conformities

- Certification **can** be granted as long as the certification body accepts the audit client’s corrective action plans.

## Planning the Closing Meeting

### 1. Mandatory Requirement

- The closing meeting is **required** for every certification audit.

### 2. Invitees

- Ensure that management of the audited areas attends.

### 3. Plan in Advance

- Schedule early, confirm the time and invitees during initial contact.
- Repeat details during the opening meeting.

### 4. Timing

- Should be the final part of the audit, held on the last day.
- Lasts around **30-60 minutes**, depending on findings.

### 5. Preparation

- Complete all audit activities.
- Draw conclusions and prepare the presentation in advance.

---

## Holding an Effective Closing Meeting

### 1. Presentation Tools

- Use **PowerPoint** or similar software.
- Incorporate **photographs** to illustrate findings.

### 2. Structure of the Presentation

#### a. Introduction

- Introduce the audit team.
- Explain audit objectives, scope, and criteria.

#### b. Audit Evidence

- Clarify that findings are based on sampling and may have some uncertainty.

#### c. Positives

- Highlight **effective** areas in the management system.

#### d. Opportunities for Improvement

- Discuss potential enhancements for the organization.

#### e. Non-Conformities

- Present non-conformities **factually** and **objectively**.

#### f. Audit Conclusions

- Explain how conclusions were drawn and classify non-conformities as **major** or **minor**.

### 3. Next Steps

- Explain:
    - Certification body’s **process and timeline** for the audit report.
    - Steps for **addressing non-conformities**.
    - Post-audit **certification review** process.
    - Appeal or **complaint process**.
    - **Confidentiality** reminder.

---

## Dealing with Feedback at Closing Meetings

### 1. Stay Calm and Focused

- If disagreements arise, stay factual and professional.

### 2. Address New Information

- If new evidence is provided, clarify that it was **not available** during the audit but can be considered later.

### 3. Document Disagreements

- Include concerns in the audit report.
- Explain the **appeal or dispute** process.

### 4. Maintain a Neutral Tone

- Be factual and polite, but not apologetic.

### 5. No Non-Conformities?

- If none are found, praise the organization for their **achievements**.

## Effective Audit Report Writing

### 1. Basic Information

- Certification body and accreditation number.
- Audit client’s **name and address**.
- Type of audit (e.g., **initial, stage 1, stage 2, surveillance, recertification**).
- Audit **objectives, scope, and criteria**.
- Audit **dates, locations, and departments**.
- Details of **lead auditor, audit team, and specialists**.

### 2. Audit Findings

- **Non-conformities, opportunities for improvement, and supporting evidence**.
- **Audit conclusions** and how criteria were met.

### 3. Audit Methodology

- Note if **remote auditing techniques** were used.
- Document any **disagreements** with findings.

### 4. Report Writing Guidelines

- Be **clear, factual, and objective**.
- Avoid opinions or directives.
- Do not use "should" or "must"; keep the report **neutral**.
- Avoid **offering solutions**—only highlight areas for improvement.

### 5. Client Responsibility

- The audit client must **analyze results, identify root causes, and develop corrective actions**.

---

## Post-Audit Activities, Corrections, and Corrective Actions

### 1. Audit Report

- Written and issued **shortly after the audit**.

### 2. Root Cause Analysis

- Audit client must **identify root causes** and take corrective actions.

### 3. Review by Lead Auditor

- Lead auditor **reviews proposed actions** to ensure suitability.

### 4. Handling Minor Non-Conformities

- Reviewed during the **next audit**.

### 5. Handling Major Non-Conformities

- Actions must be accepted and verified.
- Special audit may be required.

### 6. Verification of Corrective Actions

- Actions must **effectively resolve** non-conformities.
- Example: If a **risk register** was created, it must include all relevant aspects.

### 7. Special Audit or Next Planned Audit

- Verification may occur during a **follow-up or next scheduled audit**.

---

## Certification Review Process

### 1. Audit Report Submission

- Report and supporting evidence sent to the **certification reviewer**.

### 2. Certification Reviewer’s Role

- Evaluates compliance with the certification body’s criteria.

### 3. Review Decision

- Reviewer may:
    - Uphold the lead auditor’s recommendation.
    - Question the recommendation.
    - Overturn the recommendation.

### 4. Outcome

- Decision determines whether **certification is granted or denied**.
